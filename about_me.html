About Me

I am a graduate of Chemical and Petroleum Engineering at the University of Calgary with a strong interest in solving complex energy challenges through analytical thinking, engineering rigor, and data-sriven decision-making. My academic and professional journey sits at the intersection of petroleum engineering, data science, and sustainable energy systems, where I focus on turning complex information into practical, real-world solutions.

I hold a foundation in reservoir engineering, subsurface production optimization, and enhanced oil recovery, complemented by hands-on experience in statistical modeling, data analytics, and technical reporting. Through roles in reservoir engineering, energy equity research, and data science, I have worked on projects that span field performance evaluation, energy access analysis, and digital system optimization. These experiences have strengthened my ability to adapt to new environments, collaborate across disciplines, and deliver results in technically demanding and fast-paced settings.

I thrive in collaborative environments where diverse perspectives lead to stronger outcomes, and I value integrity, curiosity, and continuous improvement in both professional and personal development. As I progress in my career, I aspire to grow into leadership roles where I can contribute to high-impact energy projects, influence strategic decisions, and help shape resilient and responsible energy systems.
